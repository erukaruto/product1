Questions about customer profiles

Where does the data come from?

From your billing provider, plus CSV imports and API events if you use them.

How is the health score calculated?

It starts at 85 and moves with each signal, such as a failed payment or no logins for 14 days. Every signal is shown.

Can I add notes?

Yes. Internal notes are visible to your team only.
