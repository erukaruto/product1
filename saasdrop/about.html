About

We build tools for teams who would rather read numbers than rebuild spreadsheets

Beacon started as an internal tool. Our founders ran a small software company and spent the first days of every month reconciling billing exports. The numbers never matched, and every meeting started with a debate about which sheet was right.

So we wrote a script that pulled the data, applied one definition of MRR and showed the result on a single page. Other founders asked for it. That script became Beacon.

Today we are a remote team of eight. We keep the product focused: clear revenue numbers, alerts that matter, and nothing you have to maintain.

How we work

Show the math

Every number in Beacon links to the records behind it. If you can't check it, we haven't finished building it.

Fewer, better alerts

An alert should lead to an action. We would rather send one useful email than ten you learn to ignore.

Your data stays yours

Export everything at any time. If you leave, we delete your data within 30 days.
