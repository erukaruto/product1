Questions about mrr and growth reports

What counts as MRR?

The normalized monthly price of every active subscription on the last day of the month, after discounts and before tax.

Do yearly plans count?

Yes. A yearly plan counts as one twelfth of its price each month.

How far back can I look?

As far as your billing history goes. Starter keeps 12 months, Growth 3 years, Scale everything.
