Your revenue data is sensitive. These are the practices behind Beacon.

Encryption

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest.

Read-only billing access

Beacon asks your billing provider for read access only. It can't charge customers or change settings.

Two-factor and SSO

Every user can turn on two-factor. Scale workspaces can require it, or sign in through SSO.

Roles

Owner, admin, billing and viewer roles limit who can export data or change settings.

Audit log

Sign-ins, exports and setting changes are recorded with the member and IP address.

Export and deletion

Export everything at any time. After cancellation, data is deleted within 30 days.

Report a security issue

If you think you've found a vulnerability, email us before sharing it publicly. We reply within two business days and keep you updated until it's fixed.
