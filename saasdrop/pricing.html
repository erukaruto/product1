Billing questions

Do I need a card for the trial?

No. Add a payment method when you pick a plan at the end of the trial.

What counts as tracked MRR?

The total monthly recurring revenue across your connected billing accounts, averaged over the last 30 days.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes. Upgrades apply right away. Downgrades apply at the start of your next billing period.

Do you offer discounts for nonprofits or students?

Yes. Contact us with a short description of your project.
