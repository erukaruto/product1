Summary

We collect what we need to run Beacon, we don't sell personal data, and you can export or delete your data at any time.

What we collect

  • Account details: name, email address, company and role.
  • Billing data you connect: customers, subscriptions, invoices and payments from your billing provider.
  • Usage data: pages viewed and features used, to improve the product.

How we use it

To provide reports and alerts, to support you when you ask for help, to send service emails, and to keep the service secure.

Who we share it with

We use a small number of service providers for hosting, email delivery and support. They process data only on our instructions. A current list is available on request.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is active. After you cancel, it stays available for export for 30 days and is then deleted from our systems, and from backups within 90 days.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask to see, correct, export or delete your personal data, or object to how we use it. Write to us and we will reply within 30 days.

Cookies

We use cookies to keep you signed in, and browser storage to remember settings such as your color theme. We don't use advertising cookies.

Contact

Privacy questions: privacy@beacon.example.
