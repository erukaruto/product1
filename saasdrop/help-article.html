Connect your billing provider

Updated Oct 14, 2026 · 3 min read

Beacon reads subscriptions, invoices and payments from your billing provider. The connection is read-only: Beacon can't charge customers or change your billing settings.

Before you start

  • You need admin access to your billing provider.
  • You need the Admin role in Beacon. Viewers can't add connections.

Steps

  1. Open Settings, then Billing connections. Choose Add connection.
  2. Pick your provider and sign in when asked. Approve read-only access.
  3. Choose the history to import. We recommend 24 months, so cohort reports have enough data.
  4. Wait for the import. You can use Beacon while older data loads. Most accounts finish within an hour.
Several accounts or regions?

Add one connection per account. Beacon converts every currency to your reporting currency using the daily exchange rate.

If the import fails

Most failures come from expired access. Open the connection and choose Reconnect. If it still fails, contact support with the error shown on screen.
